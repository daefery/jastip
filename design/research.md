# UI/UX references for Jastip (30 Oct 2026)

Each lesson is taken from a product that solves one part of our flow. Sources at the end of each row.

| Our moment | Best reference | Lesson to copy |
|---|---|---|
| Traveller in the shop | Instacart Shopper app | Built for one hand: big targets, big images, bold prompts, no typing. The shopper's other hand holds a basket. ([Instacart design](https://company.instacart.com/tech-innovation/designing-digital-experiences-that-augment-the-analog-world)) |
| Item sold out | Instacart substitutions | The shopper scans the replacement, the app writes the message, the customer approves in one tap with a photo. The friend sets a "if sold out" rule when asking, because the shop moment is the worst time to decide. ([Instacart design](https://company.instacart.com/tech-innovation/designing-digital-experiences-that-augment-the-analog-world), [teardown](https://productguys.arthakram.in/teardowns/instacart-substitutions)) |
| Final price unknown until the basket | Instacart receipt | Show the change against the estimate; list substitutions and refunds separately from items delivered as asked. ([teardown](https://productguys.arthakram.in/teardowns/instacart-substitutions)) |
| Friends without the app | Monzo Split | A web guest experience: friends who don't bank with Monzo still pay from a link. ([Monzo Split, Katie Hudak](https://katiehudak.com/project/monzo-split)) |
| Whose money is this? | Dino (group payments, 2026 redesign) | "Every number says whose money it is, and every screen has one obvious thing to do next." One main action per screen. ([Usarion case study](https://www.usarion.com/work/dino/)) |
| Owes / is owed | Splitwise | Green = money coming to you, red = money you owe; the settle-up screen puts two avatars on either side of an arrow so the money's direction is visible. ([Pratt IXD critique, Feb 2026](https://ixd.prattsi.org/2026/02/design-critique-splitwise-mobile-app/)) |
| Group already exists | WhatsApp / iMessage groups | Jastip lives in group chats today (pasabuy and jastip posts in Facebook/WhatsApp groups); the chat is the natural home for asks, swaps and receipts. |

## What not to copy
- Instacart: the total assembles across several screens (drip pricing). Show the fee and PayPal's fee up front, once. ([teardown](https://productguys.arthakram.in/teardowns/instacart-substitutions))
- Splitwise: summary text that looks tappable but isn't ("Plus 2 other balances"). ([Pratt IXD](https://ixd.prattsi.org/2026/02/design-critique-splitwise-mobile-app/))
